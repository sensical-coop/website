Test benches, laboratories and simulation teams produce more data every year, and much of it still reaches a report through copied spreadsheets and manual steps. We study {{< accent >}}how your engineers actually turn data into results{{< /accent >}}, and move that work to templates, formula libraries and automated sequences that are validated against your existing results, documented, and maintained by your own team.

{{< alert type="bg-sensical" >}}

### The problem

A typical evaluation workflow grows one workbook at a time: a template per test bench, formulas copied between projects, a macro that only one person understands, and a report rebuilt by hand for every test. It works until a formula is fixed in one copy and not the others, a new test system changes the file format, or the person who built it leaves. By then, nobody is sure which version of a calculation is the right one.

{{</alert>}}

## How we work

We start from what exists, not from a blank page. A migration that changes the results engineers trust, or stops testing for a month, is not a success.

1. **Understand the workflow**: follow the data from the measurement system to the final report, and talk to the engineers who use it.
2. **Inventory it**: data sources and formats, calculations, report templates, scripts and the tools around them.
3. **Find the risks**: manual steps, duplicated formulas, version problems, synchronisation issues and checks that depend on one person.
4. **Decide what to keep**: existing calculations and report layouts are often the result of years of engineering decisions.
5. **Rebuild it**: calculations become formula libraries, reports become templates, and repetitive steps become scripts and sequences.
6. **Validate it**: the new workflow is run on reference data from before the migration and the results are compared.
7. **Hand it over**: training, documentation and a staged rollout, so engineers can maintain the workflow themselves.

## Who it is for

+ **Test departments and laboratories** (powertrain, emissions, batteries, components) whose evaluation lives in spreadsheets.
+ **Engineering service companies** that produce reports for many customers and projects from the same kind of data.
+ **Teams changing their test or data systems** that need their evaluation and reports to follow without interrupting testing.

## Our offer

+ Workflow analysis: a map of how data becomes results today, with the risks and the manual work it involves
+ A target workflow and a staged migration plan
+ Formula libraries generated from existing calculation sheets, with units and naming conventions per project
+ Report templates that reproduce, or improve, the reports your engineers and customers already use
+ Automation: scripts and sequences that load data, compute, and produce PDF, PowerPoint, Word or Excel reports in one step
+ Validation against reference results, and support while the old and new workflows run side by side
+ Training for engineers, and documentation they can follow

## Domain knowledge

Engine and vehicle test benches, emission measurement and regulatory evaluation (see our [emission computation](/projects/emission-computation/) project), battery cell testing, calibration data and simulation results. Data formats such as MDF4, CSV, NetCDF and Excel, test-data databases such as ASAM ODS, and the scripting and automation features of analysis tools such as [UniPlot](/projects/uniplot/).

The workflow belongs to your engineers. We build it with them, using the tools they already have where possible, so that they can change a formula or a template without calling us. It is the same approach we take in our [co-creation](/services/co-creation/) work.
