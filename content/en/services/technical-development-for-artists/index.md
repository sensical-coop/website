We develop the technology behind performances and art installations, from {{< accent >}}stage lighting and wearables to robotics and interactive systems{{< /accent >}}. We build it with the artists, make it reliable and robust to tour, and train the team to run it without us.

## Iron Skulls Co

For the past ten years, our team has worked with the dance company [Iron Skulls Co](https://ironskulls.es/en/) on the technology in their shows. Iron Skulls Co has developed its own language, Theatrical Breakin', which combines breaking with influences from other dance styles and disciplines and, increasingly, with light and technology on stage. Their work moves between the street and the theatre, balancing grace and disruption.

## Light design

Our work with Iron Skulls Co includes wearables, light design and displays, and robotics. For [Azul Petróleo](/projects/azul-petroleo-masks/), we built wearable masks controlled wirelessly and live from the lighting desk, combining addressable LEDs, high-power LEDs and lasers. For the same show, we developed remotely controlled E1.31 LED sets for the stage design, and brought the whole show control into a system that a single person can operate.

## Robotics

For a later show, [Famulus 4.0](/projects/famulus-4-0/), we developed a system to program and control a Unitree Go2 robot dog on stage. It connects to the robot wirelessly, and takes input from a game controller and a MIDI controller, so that an operator can steer the robot live during the performance.

## How we work

As in all our [co-creation](/services/co-creation/) work, we work hands-on with artists and makers. They shape the solutions we develop, and gain the skills to keep using them and to keep working with new technologies.

**Bringing technology to a show is demanding**: it has to work every night, in every venue, and it can't fail. We have field experience in lighting design and control, robotics and stage design, and we build {{< accent >}}together{{< /accent >}} with you, so that you can tour with the result and set it up on your own.

## What we offer

+ Turnkey projects that bring technology into the performing arts and art installations
