Óscar González is an Industrial Engineer and researcher based in Barcelona with expertise in data science, sensor development, and calibration through his experience in the automotive sector and in the Smart Citizen project. Óscar works as a multidisciplinary researcher on various projects, spanning the development and implementation of open source technologies and low-cost sensors, including their application in citizen science initiatives, as tools for environmental and health assessment, and the interoperability of data across research infrastructures. Óscar is co-founder of Sensical SCCL. He is  also a creative engineer and technical director at Iron Skulls Co, contemporary dance company based in Barcelona, working in the field of  applied robotics, interaction and stage design in dance performances.
