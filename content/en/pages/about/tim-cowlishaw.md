Tim is an interaction designer and researcher with 20 years of experience designing and developing products, services, and experiences for the web and digital media, 9 of which he has spent as a specialist in R&D and prototyping, including 3 years in the BBC's R&D department in London. He holds a bachelor’s degree in moving image design, and a master's degree in computer science, and is currently a PhD candidate in the DARTS (Design, Arts and Technoscience) group at the Open University of Catalonia (UOC), where he is researching the environmental impact of digital media.
