Adai Surinach is trained in Fine Arts with specializations in engraving and printmaking techniques at the Escola Llotja in Barcelona. Later he went on to specialize in 3D printing and electronic hardware design. He has been part of the Smart Citizen project since 2023 and is an instructor on the FabAcademy program at Fab Lab Barcelona. He also collaborates on artistic projects with various brands and designers.
