Rebecca Anne Peters is a researcher and artist originally from the United States. In 2021 she received her doctorate in Humanities from Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona with a thesis on technology’s role within collective cultural narratives. She completed a master’s degree in Psychosocial Studies at Birkbeck, University of London (2014) as well as a master’s degree in Fine Arts at Goldsmiths, University of London (2013). Her academic background combines cultural studies, psychology, art, and technology, which gives her a unique interdisciplinary perspective about open source principles and alternative models as well as their impact on technology and society.
