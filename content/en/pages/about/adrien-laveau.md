Adrien is a mechanical engineer specialised in powertrain development. His experience in engine calibration led him to work with numerous sensors, measurement systems, and datasets, this progressively sparked his interest in tools that transform data into useful information for engineering and decision-making. He is currently responsible for the development of a data analysis software and its ecosystem, combining software development, data engineering and measurement systems with product development, marketing, and customer relations. His experience at the FabLab has also given him skills in digital fabrication, electronic design, and rapid prototyping.
